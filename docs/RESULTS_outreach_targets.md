# Outreach target lists from the corpus authorships

Generated by `scripts/08_outreach_targets.py`. Every number below is computed and printed by that script. These are research leads. This note holds no contact details.

## Data and identity

- Papers are the merged records of `scripts/06_citation_bridge.py` (same loading, same `build_gmap`). 2,616 distinct ids (2,654 entries in the two raw files, 38 extra entries from repeated ids) became 2,458 merged papers. Of these, 1,385 are in the polycrisis corpus, 1,080 in the LtG corpus and 7 in both.
- An author is an OpenAlex author id. Names are never used to match people. The name shown is the one most often seen for that id.
- A merged paper takes the authorships of all its raw records, joined by author id, so an author counts once per merged paper. Year and citation count are those of the merged paper's most cited record, as in 06.
- Authorships read: 5,782. Authorships with no author id: 694. These are left out of every list.
- Merged papers with no usable author id: 254.
- Distinct author ids: 2,848 in the polycrisis corpus, 1,419 in the LtG corpus, 4,239 in either.
- Institutions are the institution names OpenAlex gives on the authorships. The CSV files list all of them; the tables below show up to 3, most frequent first.

## Basis of the figures

Total citations, latest year and institutions seen are taken from a different set of papers in each list. B1 and B3: the author's polycrisis papers. B2: the author's papers in either corpus. B4: the author's papers in the target journals in the 2024 to 2026 window only.

## B3. Central authors in the polycrisis coauthor network

Method: The network has one node per author id in the polycrisis corpus and one edge between two authors who share at least one merged polycrisis paper. It has 2,848 nodes and 10,614 edges in 1,028 connected components; the largest has 202 nodes. Degree is the number of distinct coauthors. Betweenness is the networkx value for the unweighted graph (version 3.2.1), normalised, computed exactly. The list is ranked by betweenness, then degree, then author id in text order. The largest number of author ids on one paper is 49. Edges join only authors who have an author id, so authorships without an id add no edges. Every paper adds a full set of edges among its authors with an id, with no cap, so a long author list adds many edges. networkx 3.2.1 is required to run the script; the script stops with a message if networkx is missing. Betweenness is rounded to 8 decimals before ranking, so authors whose values differ by less than that count as tied and are then ordered by degree and author id.

Counts: 6 authors are in both the B1 and B3 lists. 13 authors have the same degree and rounded betweenness as rank 15, at ranks 15 to 27; the table shows all of them, ordered only by author id. 0 other authors are tied with rank 30. Of the top 30, 16 authors are coauthors on the 2 papers with the longest author list (49 author ids each): W4414512012, W7125682156. File: `data/outreach_B3.csv`.

## B4. Authors in the target journals, 2024 to 2026

Method: the target journals are the four journals chosen for this list (from the outreach plan): Global Sustainability, Scientometrics, Sustainability, Journal of Industrial Ecology. A paper matches when the source display name of its primary location equals one of these names exactly, for any raw record of the merged paper, in either corpus, with a publication year from 2024 to 2026. The year is the merged paper's year (the year of its most cited record), not each raw record's own year. 30 merged papers (counted over all their raw records, in both corpora) have records with differing years; the set of matched papers is the same if each record's own year is used instead. Rank authors by papers in this set, then total citations, then author id. The columns In polycrisis and In LtG say whether the author has any paper in that corpus, not necessarily a target journal paper.

Source names that contain a target name as a case insensitive substring but are not an exact match, and so were not used (papers in range): Frontiers in Sustainability (2); Nature Sustainability (1); SCI Sustainability (1); Strategies for sustainability (1); Sustainability Science (4); Sustainability Science Practice and Policy (1); World sustainability series (2).

Counts: 30 merged papers and 92 authors. 2 papers add no author because no authorship on it has an author id (W4410237573 (0 authorships on record), W7111291106 (6 authorships on record)). File: `data/outreach_B4.csv`.

## Ties

A full tie means every sort key before the author id is equal. Where a tie crosses the end of a 15 row table or the cut at 30, the rows beyond the cut have the same keys as the last row shown. The B3 table prints the whole tie at rank 15; the other tables stop at rank 15, and the CSV files hold the full lists (B1 and B3 hold the top 30).

| List | Order (each step applies only when the earlier ones tie) | Full tie at rank 15 | Full tie at rank 30 |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | papers, then total citations, then author id | no tie | 3 tied, ranks 28 to 30 |
| B2 | smaller of the two corpus counts, then total papers, then total citations, then author id | 6 tied, ranks 14 to 19 | list ends before this rank |
| B3 | betweenness rounded to 8 decimals, then degree, then author id | 13 tied, ranks 15 to 27 | no tie |
| B4 | papers in the target journals, then total citations, then author id | 8 tied, ranks 9 to 16 | 20 tied, ranks 26 to 45 |

## Limitations

- OpenAlex author disambiguation makes errors. One person can be split across several ids, which lowers that person's counts. Two people can share one id, which raises them. Nothing here corrects this.
- 694 of 5,782 authorships have no author id and are not counted.
- The corpora are keyword searches, not the whole literature. 1,385 polycrisis papers and 1,080 LtG papers say nothing about authors who write on these topics under other terms. Counts describe the corpora only.
- B3 is built from polycrisis papers only. Coauthor links that exist only through LtG papers are not in the network. Papers with long author lists add many edges, and the primary list applies no cap; the sensitivity table shows the effect of a cap of 20.
- Ties are common in every list. The order inside a full tie comes from the author id, which carries no meaning. See the Ties section.
- Institution names come from OpenAlex affiliation matching and are not checked. The note tables show up to 3 institutions per author and the CSV files list all of those seen, on the papers described under Basis of the figures. Treat them as unverified.
- B2 depends on the 7 papers that are in both corpora, as described above.
- The script needs these files in `data/`: polycrisis_papers_raw.json, ltg_refined_papers_raw.json, polycrisis_papers.csv, ltg_refined_papers.csv, references_refetch_2026-10-24.json and canon_metadata_cache.json (bridge_papers.csv is optional). `data/` is gitignored, so a clean clone must rebuild them (scripts 01 to 06) before this script can run.
- B4 uses the primary location only. A paper listed under another source name is not matched. Journal names were matched exactly as written, so renamed or abbreviated forms are missed.
- Citation totals are OpenAlex counts at the time of the data pull and are summed over an author's papers in the list.
- These are research leads, not a contact list. The script reads no email address, phone number or personal page, and none is stored in the outputs.
